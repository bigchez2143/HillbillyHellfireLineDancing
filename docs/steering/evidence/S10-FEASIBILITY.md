Checked 3 September 2026. Public documentation does not prove authenticated access, granted publishing rights or working paid integrations. No account credentials, paid calls or hosted deployments were used. The product contract remains [Integrations and release](../INTEGRATIONS-AND-RELEASE.md); S11/S12 depend on the gates below.

## Operating model

The core remains local/offline without AI. AI uses a user's provider/account/local endpoint and their charges. No developer/shared key, credit pool, company-funded hosted inference, AI proxy or hidden fallback is allowed. Local speech/stem modules are optional user-installed/user-managed tools. A proposed public service must identify an operator, normal hosting costs, moderation and privacy responsibilities without introducing an app-owned AI service.

## Freeware and publication gates

The owner's policy intent prohibits selling/monetizing the software while allowing users to make dances and earn money from teaching, events and dance outputs. Write this exception clearly so a paid class using the tool does not breach a blanket commercial-use prohibition. Resolve software bundling/resale, paid support, modified distribution and hosted access before final terms. A no-sale restriction does not meet OSI's redistribution/commercial-use conditions. This is policy tracking, not a final licence or legal certification. [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd)

Retain all common catalog moves and truthful private history; publish cleared assets and independently authored text. Keep source/date/creator/permission records for imports and submissions. Software terms cannot grant rights in third-party recordings, dances, videos or service data.

Windows/GitHub gates: stable source revision; clean allowlisted tree; no credentials/projects/private research; dependency/native notices and retained wheel hashes; build checksums; storage/upgrade/recovery validation; clean-machine offline/non-admin run; visual export and instructor acceptance; final terms/privacy; known repository owner/name/visibility and tagged assets. Signing is separate: MSIX needs trusted signing and no certificate has been acquired. No public repository/release was created here. [Microsoft signing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/msix/package/signing-package-overview), [GitHub releases](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/releasing-projects-on-github/managing-releases-in-a-repository)
