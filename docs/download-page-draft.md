# Line Dance Creator

## What it is

A free Windows app from Hillbilly Hellfire for writing, rehearsing, and teaching line dances.

You can start a dance, add moves, practice to a count, export a step sheet, and open a class with the one-page guide inside the download. A starter dance, The Corn Maze Dance, is included. No song file is included. Use a recording you have permission to use when you want music.

## Download

Download the Windows zip. There is no store account and no installer.

1. Download `HillbillyHellfire-LineDanceCreator-windows.zip`.
2. Unzip it. Keep the folder as it is.
3. Double-click **Launch Line Dance Creator.bat**. **run.bat** in that same folder starts the same way.
4. Your browser opens the dance page on this computer (`http://127.0.0.1:8766/dance`).
5. Open **Instructor one-pager.pdf** in the same folder when you want the one-page class guide.

There is no Setup.exe and no desktop icon to install. If Windows asks you to trust the extracted files, that is the zip itself, not a separate installer.

Replace this paragraph with the real file link when the GitHub Release asset is attached.

## No account required

You do not create an account to use Line Dance Creator. You do not sign in to write, practice, or publish a step sheet from this app. Publish saves the sheet on your computer and opens BootStepper, CopperKnob, or LineDance.com only when you choose to. You upload the sheet yourself.

## Bring your own keys, at no charge from us

The dance tools are zero-cost. Optional connections, if you want them, are also zero-cost inside this app: bring your own credentials and pay that provider yourself if they charge you. This download does not include a shared AI account, a shared key, or paid credits. Leave those blank. The app still works.

## Freeware, including paid classes

You may use the app in paid classes, workshops, and performances, and you may charge for your own choreography and teaching. Do not sell the app or charge people for access to it. Dances you create stay yours. Songs and other people's sheets stay under their own terms. The terms are in the `LICENSE` file inside the zip. The Software is provided as is.

This is freeware for the line dance community. It is not an OSI open source license.
