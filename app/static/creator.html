    SETTINGS / HELP

    Make yourself at home.

    Community links

    Step-sheet sites and your own class pages live with the other class tools. Links only.

    A simple place to begin

    1. Start with or without a song.
    2. Add moves and arrange your dance order.
    3. Save your draft, then practice and export.

    Basic and Advanced show the same project. Advanced adds production controls; it does not change the dance difficulty.

    Drafts save after you stop editing. Use Versions to keep a named milestone. Saving keeps your working draft separate from the accepted version. Older production tools support flat legacy sequences.

    Your connections

    You can write, practice, and publish with no keys. Speech and stem models are optional local add-ons. Personal keys, when you want them, are under Advanced in Workspace view.

    Advanced

    Advanced is optional. Leave it empty. Writing a dance, practice, Publish, and the song card all work with no keys.

    A personal key stays on this Windows account. It is not shared, it is not included in a dance export, and Publish does not ask for it. Publish still only saves a step sheet and a portable draft, then opens a website.

    BootStepper search uses a personal key from your own BootStepper account. Search is read-only. This app does not upload dances and does not keep a copy of BootStepper’s catalog.

    Bring your own AI is the same Optional AI settings as before. You pay that provider. This project is not billed.

    BootStepper

    Search dances, songs, and choreographers with a personal key from your own account. Read only. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing from the search is saved into your dance.

    Create a personal key on BootStepper. Their terms are at bootstepper.com/terms. Use your own key. Do not share it, and do not build a copy of their catalog.

    Bring your own AI

    This is the same optional connection as before. The key stays on this computer and is not sent with a dance.

    Spotify

    A Spotify client id is not enabled. Policy for that connection is not cleared, so this screen does not ask for one. The song card still uses a share link only.
